## Findings (3D), ranked by what a viewer would notice

1. **The camera looks elsewhere than the video.** The sim's yaw/pitch are the puppets' own (scripted aim or the
   direction of travel), not the recording's: at the Lokier duel (18.5 s) the video looks out of the Apartments window
   at B, the 3D shot looks at the opening beside it; donk (33.4-37.4 s) faces a wall while the video looks down the
   Apartments corridor. Cause: round.json has no view angles. Fix: V2's per-frame yaw/pitch fit into the tracks, and
   the puppets' look following it while spectated. Owner: V2 (data), then the core's look schedule.
2. **donk's AWP scope is not shown (33.4-37.4 s).** The video is scoped in (black scope overlay, zoomed FOV) for most of
   donk's camera time; the 3D app shows him unscoped. Lowest similarity of the run (F 0.19-0.29). Cause: no zoom state
   in the data or the puppets. Fix: a scope timeline (from the video: the overlay is easy to detect) and the scoped
   FOV/overlay in the 3D view while spectating. Owner: round data (timeline) + H1 (render).
3. **The defuser is in plain sight of cart at 66 s.** In the video cart sprays at the B-site cylinder stack with
   Sp4rtaN defusing out of view; in the 3D app Sp4rtaN crouches on the bomb in the open, just left of that stack, in
   cart's view. Cause: Sp4rtaN's defuse spot (−2005, 345) is where his radar dot went under the C4 icon (not an
   observation of where he crouched), and/or the plant spot (see the V0 data-fix PR). Fix: place the defuser where
   the video hides him from cart (behind the stack, within 62 u of the bomb), once V2 has the view angles to check it.
   Owner: round data.
4. **The view metric needs a reference to mean anything.** Fix (V0, next): score against a shuffled-timestamp floor
   (as above) and report the margin; compare edges only inside a depth-masked region (the 3D app can render a depth or
   silhouette pass) or on labelled key frames (`tools/parity/labels-3d.json`, `docs/parity.md`). Owner: V0.
5. **The 3D app's HUD differs from CS:GO's** (bomb countdown, a status panel, the radar in the top-left at another
   size). Masked out of the metric; for "looks the same", copy CS:GO's HUD layout. Owner: H1/U1. Low.
