# V1 · End-to-end CS2-fidelity audit, 2026-11-07

Tester: V1 (docs/sim3d-plan.md). Branch `v1-e2e-tests`, main at `eefd85a` merged. Bar: a seasoned CS2 player should not
be able to tell movement and shooting apart from **competitive-mode CS2 defaults** (no KZ settings). Everything was
measured through the 3D host (`Sim3DHost`, driven like the 3D app: `setHumanInput` + `tick`), the browser app
(Playwright), or both. The scripts are in `tools/e2e/`, and you can re-run them with `npm run test:e2e:*` (below). The
numbers come from a shared 2-core box at load 8–20, so the browser frame rates are lower bounds.

## The AK-47 (John 11:02): is it slow?

**No in the mechanics.** Per shot at 500 u on 100 armor + helmet the AK does 109 head / 27 chest / 34 stomach / 27 arm / 26 legs. That is
CS2's formula with weapons.vdata's numbers: damage 36, `m_flArmorRatio` 1.55 → 77.5 % through armor, HS ×4, range modifier 0.98 per 500 u.
CS2 uses ×1.25 for the **stomach** only. Chest and arms are ×1 and legs ×0.75 (docs/weapons.md S1, S7: the recorded AK hits
27 / 34 / 26 reproduce). It fires 600 RPM: cycle 0.1 s, ticks 0, 7, 13, 20 (+0.0016 s mean from tick quantization). The
headless TTK is the CS2 one: 4 chest bullets = **313 ms** with a pull-down, 1 headshot = instant. Uncontrolled, the 3rd bullet climbs into
the head (203 ms) or over it (73 / 300 seeds don't kill with 3–4 bullets).

**What makes it feel slow in the app** is, in order: (1) the recorded-duel block (finding 2). Shooting Lokier at the window
or sp4rtan in his duels does nothing until the tape's tick, and that is exactly the takeover moment the browser check uses.
(2) A starved browser runs the whole game in slow motion (finding 3). In game time the AK still fires 10 shots per
64 ticks. (3) Hit registration is not the cause: hits land on the hitbox they enter, and the damage events equal the formula.

## Raw numbers

### 1. AK-47 sprays and accuracy (`npm run test:e2e:spray`, 1,000 seeds)

The per-bullet mean pattern is `data/weapons.json`'s, generated from CS2's `m_nRecoilSeed` and recoil parameters
(docs/weapons.md). The only public CS2 per-weapon numbers are csstats.gg's envelope (AK 11.06° × 7.14°), which matches,
and csdb.gg's shape description ("climbs almost straight up for roughly the first 9–10 bullets, then drifts left before
swinging back to the right"). The pattern matches it: bullets 2–5 have |yaw| < 0.25°, bullet 9 is at −9.97°, bullets 10–16 drift left
(+yaw) up to 4.3°, then go back right. No public per-bullet CS2 table with provenance exists.
