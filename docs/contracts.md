# Contracts

## IHost / ICore (Core ↔ engine, in-process)

Observe (engine → Core, every tick):
- `tick`, `t`
- per pawn (array sorted by id): `id, team, pos{x,y,z}, vel, yaw, pitch, hp, armor, alive, weapon, ammo{clip,reserve}, crouching, sees: ids[]` (`sees` = line of sight and within FOV, engine-computed; exact definition under Types, "sees")
- events since last tick: `damage {attacker, victim, amount, armor, weapon, hitgroup, scripted}`, `shot {shooter, weapon}`, `death {victim, attacker, weapon, headshot}`, `sound {source, pos, kind}`, `bomb {planted|defuse_start|defuse_stop|defused|exploded}`, `round_end {winner, reason}`, (G5) `blocked_hit {attacker, victim, weapon, hitgroup}` (a bullet of the human's that `block_damage` stopped: whom it would have hit, and where)
- `human: {id | null}` — which pawn the human controls (G4: null while the player watches; it becomes the pawn on the tick he takes control of it)
- `round` (C3): `{phase: live|planted|over, score {ct, t}, winner, reason, endTick, timeLeftSec, bomb {state: carried|dropped|planted|defused|exploded, carrier, pos, plantTick, defuser, defuseEndTick}}` — round state and the C4 (CS2: game rules and the `planted_c4` entity)

Act (Core → engine, same tick):
- `inputs[]`: `{id, forward, side, yaw, pitch, buttons[]}` for every REPLAY pawn (`forward/side` in −1..1 relative to the pawn's yaw; buttons `ATTACK, ATTACK2, USE, DUCK, WALK, JUMP, RELOAD`)
- `actions[]`:
  - `damage {attacker, victim, amount, armor, hitgroup, weapon, lethal}` — scripted damage, engine applies it exactly
  - `block_damage {attacker, victim}` / `unblock_damage` — engine must not apply its own bullet damage for that pair while blocked
  - `set_mode {id, mode: REPLAY|LIVE, goal {route: pos[], role, hold: pos}}`
  - `set_weapon {id, weapon}`, `give {id, item, clip, reserve}`, `set_hp {id, hp, armor, helmet}`
  - `teleport {id, pos, yaw}` (setup, rewinds, unseen snaps only)
  - `plant {id, pos}`, `defuse_start {id}`, `defuse_stop {id}`
  - `end_round {winner, reason}`
  - `set_round {tick, round}`, `strip {id}` (B5, resync rewind only)
  - `log {level, text}` — for the trigger/status panel
- `status`: per pawn `{mode, trigger, sinceTick}` for the UI (`mode` REPLAY, LIVE, DEAD or HUMAN, B4; `trigger` `rejoin` on a REPLAY pawn back from LIVE, G1)

Control (app → Core/engine): `start`, `restart`, `seek {t}`, `checkpoint`, `rewind {id}`, `pause`, `speed {x}`, (G4) `take_control {id}`.

Capabilities (B5, finding F6): `IHost.capabilities()` → `{snapshot, seek, speed}`, what the engine can do beyond observe / apply. The driver degrades: without `snapshot` a rewind resyncs the engine through actions, without `seek` it can't run ahead of real time, without `speed` the `speed` control is refused.

Logging: `SessionLog` writes one JSON line per tick `{observe, act}`. `Replayer` feeds a logged observe stream back into any `ICore` and diffs the act output. These logs are also the fixtures for the C# port.

The TypeScript types for all of this are defined in task A4 (`src/contracts`); this document is the human-readable spec they must match.

Details the spec left open, as decided:

- **No optional fields.** Every field is always present; "absent" is `null` (fits `exactOptionalPropertyTypes` and C# records). All fields and arrays are `readonly`.
- **Unions** are discriminated by a string `type` field whose value is the spec name (`damage`, `block_damage`, `round_end`, …). Damage *event* and damage *action* both use `type: 'damage'`; they live in different unions.
- **Spellings.** Player ids lowercase; teams `T`/`CT`; weapons `ak47 awp ssg08 deagle m4a4 glock knife c4 smoke flash he molotov` (`glock` added in B1: donk's secondary); items (`ItemId`) are the weapons plus `defuser`; buttons upper case; hitgroups `head chest stomach arm leg neck generic` (`neck` and `generic` added in C2: CS2 hitgroups 8 and 0; `generic` = no body part, used for grenade, fire and bomb damage; the 2D engine never produces `arm` or `neck`); modes `REPLAY LIVE DEAD HUMAN` (`HUMAN` added in B4). New, not in the spec: `SoundKind` = `footstep land shot reload grenade bomb`; `RoundEndReason` = `elimination bomb_exploded bomb_defused time`; `LogLevel` = `debug info warn error`.
- **Observe.** `pawns` holds all ten pawns (dead ones too), sorted by id; `human` is a `PlayerId | null` (not an object). `vel` is a `Vec3` in units/s. A dead pawn reports its last weapon. `sees` is sorted and de-duplicated. `events` keep engine emission order.
- **Event fields.** `damage.attacker`, `damage.weapon`, `death.attacker`, `death.weapon` may be `null` (world damage; the bomb's explosion is `attacker: null, weapon: null, hitgroup: generic`). `sound.source` may be `null` (C3, adapter finding F4): bomb beeps have no player (CS2 `bomb_beep` carries only the entity). `bomb` is `{phase, id, pos}`: `phase` is one of the five spec states, `id` the planter/defuser (null for `exploded`), `pos` the bomb position or null. `damage.amount` = HP removed, `damage.armor` = armor removed, both capped at what the victim had (a 111-damage headshot on 100 HP reports 100). A `death` follows the `damage` event that caused it. A blocked hit (`block_damage`) emits no `damage` event, but its `shot` and `sound` are still emitted (G5: and, for a bullet of the human's, a `blocked_hit`). Combat rules: `docs/combat.md`.
- **Round (C3).** `Observe.round: RoundObs`, always present:
  - `phase`: `live` until the plant, `planted` while the bomb ticks, `over` from the `round_end` tick on (even if the bomb still ticks).
  - `score: Score {ct, t}`: the current score; the round's win is counted from its end tick on. The engine gets the score before the round from its setup (2D: the Session passes `round.meta.score`, CT 11 – T 15).
  - `winner: Team | null`, `reason: RoundEndReason | null`, `endTick: number | null`: null until the round ends; then they repeat the `round_end` event and its tick.
  - `timeLeftSec`: the round clock while live (2D: `round.meta.clock0` − t), the bomb timer while it ticks (also after a round end); after a defuse the bomb timer at the defuse, after an explosion 0, otherwise frozen at the end tick. Never negative.
  - `bomb: BombObs`: `state` (`carried | dropped | planted | defused | exploded`); `carrier` (the pawn carrying it while `carried`; null otherwise and before anyone has it); `pos` (the carrier's position, where it lies, or the bomb; null when nobody carries it); `plantTick` (null before the plant); `defuser` and `defuseEndTick` (the defuse in progress; after `defused` who defused it and on which tick; else null).
  - A plant in progress is not in the contract. Rules (2D): `docs/combat.md`, "Bomb and round rules".
- **Angles.** `pitch` is Source's: degrees, positive = looking down, 0 = level (C2; the 2D hitscan picks the hitgroup from it, `docs/combat.md`). `yaw` is degrees counter-clockwise from +x (0 = east, 90 = north); its range is not normalized.
- **Act.** `inputs` has one entry per REPLAY pawn, sorted by id; `buttons` in `BUTTONS` order (`sortButtons`). `actions` keep Core order and the engine applies them in that order. `status` has all ten pawns sorted by id; `trigger` is a free string or null (B4: the trigger that made a LIVE pawn LIVE, one of `damage sighting gunfire outlived blocked encounter`, G4 `released`; G1: `rejoin` for a REPLAY pawn that came back from LIVE; null for other REPLAY pawns, DEAD and HUMAN); `sinceTick` is the tick the mode began. **HUMAN** (B4) is the living pawn `observe.human` names; it never appears with `human: null`.
- **set_mode** takes `REPLAY | LIVE` only (`DEAD` and `HUMAN` are status-only: `SettableMode = Exclude<Mode, 'DEAD' | 'HUMAN'>`); `goal` is `{route: Vec3[], role: string, hold: Vec3 | null}` for LIVE and `null` for REPLAY. From `set_mode LIVE` on, the **engine** drives the pawn (CS2: the native bot; 2D: `src/sim2d/ai.ts`, `docs/ai.md`) and ignores Core inputs for it; `REPLAY` hands it back. Every `set_mode LIVE` starts the goal afresh. The 2D engine's roles (C4): `hold`, `rotate`, `take_site`, `plant`, `guard_bomb`, `retake`, `defuse`, `hunt`; any other string holds.
- **sees** (C4, finding F5; G6). For each alive pawn V, every *other* alive pawn T of **either team** such that: V is not blind (flashed: `tick < blindUntilTick`); T is within `ai.fovDeg / 2` of V's yaw; no active smoke crosses the 2D segment V–T; and the line from V's eye (eye = `pos.z` + `damage.eyeHeight` 64, or `crouchEyeHeight` 46 crouched) to **any of T's aim points** is clear: head, chest, left and right side at chest height (`ai.sightSideUnits` 12 off the body axis, perpendicular to the line of sight), legs (`damage.bandHeights`; crouched × 46 / 64). Not symmetric in general. Sorted by id; dead pawns see nothing and are seen by nobody. 2D sim: "clear" is a segment against the CS2 collision mesh's world triangles (no player clips, no glass; `docs/vision.md`); without 3D geometry the radar sight mask plus a level check. CS2: engine traces to the same points with a sight mask, then the same FOV, smoke and flash tests (`docs/cs2-adapter.md`).
- **give.item** is an `ItemId` (a `WeaponId` or `defuser`, the defuse kit; B1). `clip/reserve` are the gun's magazine and reserve; for grenades, `c4` and `defuser` they are 0, and each `give` adds one item (two smoke gives = two smokes). Every pawn has a `knife` without a give.
- **set_hp** carries `helmet: boolean` (B1) next to `hp` and `armor`.
- **Control.** `checkpoint {id}` carries an app-chosen name and `rewind {id}` returns to it; `pause {paused: boolean}` is explicit (no toggle); `seek {t}` is in video seconds; `speed {x}` is a multiplier.
- **Round data.** `parseRound(json | text)` validates `data/round.json` and returns it typed as `RoundData` (readonly). It checks in depth the fields the Core uses (meta clock/bomb timer, players, tracks, shots, kills, damage, utility, radio, chat, bomb, `timelines.weapon`, `events`), including `tick === toTick(t)` for every pair, and only the container shape of the rest; it throws `RoundDataError` naming the first bad path. `TrackSample.uncertainty` and `.resampled` are the only optional fields (absent in the file); read them as nullable. `tools/*` import these types from `src/contracts` (B1 moved them from `tools/round-types.ts`).
- **Snapshots** are `{kind: 'host' | 'core', data: JsonValue}`; `data` is private to the implementation that made it. Use `toJsonValue` to build it from typed state.
- **Log line** = `stableStringify({act, controls, observe})`. `controls` (new) lists the Control messages delivered to Core since the previous tick, applied before `step`; the Replayer re-sends them. Ticks may repeat (after a rewind); `get(tick)` returns the first such line, `at(i)` any line.
- **Canonical inputs.** Logged numbers are rounded to 6 decimals, so a driver must pass `canonicalize(host.observe())` to `core.step` (or the host must already emit canonical numbers); otherwise a replay feeds Core slightly different numbers than the live run did.
- **Replayer diffs** carry the expected and actual `Act` objects (actual canonicalized), plus a total `mismatchCount`.
- **digest()** is FNV-1a 32-bit over the UTF-8 bytes of `toText()`, 8 lowercase hex digits. `toText()` ends every line with `\n`.
- **Trace.** 10 Hz samples at log ticks `Math.round(i * 6.4)`; `t` is the sample's `observe.t` (3 decimals), positions rounded to whole units, flag always `0` ("observed"); a track stops after its first dead sample. Also emits `players [{id, team, death}]`, `kills` from death events and `events` from death/bomb/round_end events and Core `log` actions (kinds `kill bomb state info`).
- **WalkQuery** (B2). `isWalkableLine(a: Vec3, b: Vec3): boolean` is true if a pawn at `a` can walk straight to `b` (every point of the segment walkable). It must be a pure function of its arguments, because the Core branches on it and a replay must take the same branches. The engine passes it to `new ReplayCore(model, schedule, tuning, seed, walk)`; `null` (the default) turns the puppets off (and G1's rejoin). 2D sim: `NavWalkQuery` (`src/sim2d/walk-query.ts`, `NavMesh.segmentWalkable`, height ignored). CS2: see `docs/port-notes.md` ("Puppets").
- **WalkQuery.pathLength** (G1). `pathLength(a: Vec3, b: Vec3): number | null`: the 2D length of the walking route the engine's nav would take from `a` to `b`, rounded to 6 decimals, or null when there is none; pure, like `isWalkableLine`. Only the rejoin planner asks it, so a run nobody leaves the tape in never calls it. 2D sim: the length of `NavMesh.path` (no one-way drops, `docs/nav.md`). CS2: `docs/port-notes.md` ("Rejoin"). The golden fixtures' walk oracle records these calls too (`paths`, `docs/golden.md`).
- **Tuning** (C4). `AiTuning` gained `losWallTolerancePx`, `losEndpointTolerancePx`, `losLevelStepUnits`, `losLevelSlackUnits` (sight, `docs/vision.md`) and `reactionJitter`, `headshotAimRatio`, `turnRateDegPerTick`, `aimSettleSec`, `aimResidualDeg`, `fireConeDeg`, `semiAutoSettleFactor`, `sprayStartFactor`, `targetLostSec`, `reloadClipFraction`, `walkNearEnemyUnits`, `memorySec`, `soundLookSec`, `arriveUnits`, `stuckSec`, `stuckProgressUnits`, `unstickSec`, `counterStrafeSpeed`, `plantSpotUnits`, `defuseRangeFactor`, `guardMaxUnits` (the 2D LIVE AI, `docs/ai.md`); all required by `parseTuning`.
- **Tuning** (B2). `ReplayTuning` gained `spikeSpeedFactor`, `spikeMaxSec`, `teleportWindowSec`, `holdLastPositionSec`, `standSpeedThreshold`, `buttonMinSec` and `steerFallbackSec` (all required by `parseTuning`; values and meaning in `docs/core.md` "Puppets").
- **Tuning** (B3). `ReplayTuning` gained `preAimSec`, `aimTurnRateDegPerTick`, `inputLatencyTicks`, `actionLatencyTicks`, `sprayGapFactor`, `throwPitchDeg`, `throwHoldSec`, `plantFallbackTicks` and `defuseFallbackTicks` (all required; `docs/core.md` "Scripted events").
- **Latency** (B3, finding F8). An event *lands* on tick t when it is in the Observe whose `tick` is t. In `Sim2DHost` an input or action in the Act of tick k shows its effect in the Observe of tick k + 1; the Core sends scripted inputs and actions `inputLatencyTicks` / `actionLatencyTicks` early. A CS2 host documents its own values (probe P3).
- **block_damage with attacker = victim** (B3) blocks a pawn's own grenade damage on itself; the Core sends it for every REPLAY puppet at round start.
- **Tuning** (B4). `ReplayTuning` gained `expectedSightingWindowSec`, `expectedGunfireWindowSec`, `encounterDelaySec`, `humanSpotToleranceUnits`, `routeThinUnits`, `offCourseUnits`, `contactSlackUnits`, `blockedSec` and `dwellUnits` (all required by `parseTuning`; values and meaning in `docs/core.md` "Divergence"). `hearingRangeUnits` and `outliveGraceSec` are now used (gunfire and outlived triggers).
- **LIVE switches** (B4). When the Core makes a pawn LIVE it sends, in this order: `unblock_damage` for every pair it blocked that involves the pawn (self pair first in id order, then both directions per enemy), `set_mode {id, mode: LIVE, goal}` and a `log` (`<id> LIVE: <reason>; goal <role>, <n> waypoints`). A scheduled encounter may send `block_damage {shooter, human}` with its scripted shot and `unblock_damage` for it afterwards: the human's pairs are otherwise never blocked.
- **Rejoin** (G1). A LIVE pawn heading back to its recording gets `set_mode {id, mode: LIVE, goal: {route: [], role: 'rejoin', hold}}` (and a `log` `<id> rejoin: heading for|chasing its track …`; possibly `set_weapon c4` for the planter); giving up re-sends its regular LIVE goal. The switch back sends, in this order: `block_damage` for its self pair and both directions with every enemy that is neither the human nor has its own blocks lifted, the reconciliation (`give` for a pickup the recording made while it was LIVE, `set_weapon`), `set_mode {id, mode: REPLAY, goal: null}` and a `log` (`<id> REPLAY: rejoined at tick <k>, …`). From then on its status is REPLAY with trigger `rejoin` and it is in `inputs` again. No new action or field: `set_mode` REPLAY existed (C4's engine drops the AI state on it).
- **Tuning** (G1). `ReplayTuning` gained `rejoinCalmSec`, `rejoinMinLiveSec`, `rejoinMinTapeSec`, `rejoinSpeedFactor`, `rejoinLeadSec`, `rejoinHorizonSec`, `rejoinLateSec` and `rejoinRetrySec` (all required by `parseTuning`, which also checks their ranges: seconds ≥ 0, horizon and retry > 0, the speed factor in (0, 1]; values and meaning in `docs/core.md` "Rejoin").
- **Capabilities** (B5). `IHost.capabilities(): HostCapabilities` = `{snapshot, seek, speed}`, constant for the host's lifetime. `snapshot`: `snapshot()` / `restore()` are exact (velocities, grenades, bomb progress, timers). `seek`: the driver may step ticks back to back, faster than real time. `speed`: the engine can run at another rate. `Sim2DHost` returns all true (`ALL_CAPABILITIES`); the **CS2 adapter returns `{snapshot: false, seek: false, speed: false}`** (`CS2_CAPABILITIES`). Then the Core and the driver: a rewind restores the Core's snapshot and **resyncs** the engine (`rewind {id}` to the Core, the checkpoint's observe to the next step, whose Act starts with the resync actions; `docs/core.md` "Checkpoints, rewind, summary"); a seek back rewinds to the latest checkpoint at or before t without a plant or defuse in progress and plays on in real time; a seek ahead and `speed` are refused; `pause` stays a driver matter.
- **Control semantics** (B5). `checkpoint {id}` (a non-empty id) and `seek {t}` (seconds ≥ 0) are driver work: the Core only checks them (a checkpoint stores `ICore.snapshot()`, and the host's with the capability; a seek steps every tick). `rewind {id}` to the Core means: the driver restored this Core to checkpoint `id` and the engine could not be restored; the next step resyncs it. With an engine snapshot the driver restores both and sends the Core nothing. The app's Session logs none of them except that `rewind`.
- **set_round** (B5, resync only): `{tick, round: RoundObs}`. The tick this Act is for: the engine relabels its clock so its next Observe reports `tick + 1`, and its round state becomes `round` (score, winner, reason, end tick; bomb state, position, plant tick, defuser and tick after a defuse). A plant or defuse in progress is not carried and not restored. Sent first in the Act. 2D: also drops grenades in flight, smokes and fires and moves per-pawn timers by the clock change; CS2: adapter tick base, `CCSGameRules` round start time, `planted_c4.C4Blow`, team scores.
- **strip** (B5, resync only): `{id}`: every item but the knife goes; the pawn holds the knife (2D: weapon timers fresh, ready at once with a teleport in the same Act; CS2: `RemoveWeapons()` and a knife). The `give`s that follow refill the inventory.
- **blocked_hit** (G5, `docs/core.md` "Recorded duels of the human"). `BlockedHitEvent {type: 'blocked_hit', attacker, victim, weapon, hitgroup}`: a bullet of the human's pawn (`observe.human`) hit `victim` while `block_damage {attacker, victim}` was in force, so the engine applied nothing (no `damage` event); the event says it would have hit, and in which hitgroup (the hitscan's band in 2D; CS2: the trace's hit group). It comes right after that shot's `shot` and `sound`, one per bullet that reached a pawn. Only the human's bullets are reported: the puppets' blocked hits (B3's round-start blocks) are not, so a run nobody plays has none, and grenades never are. The Core blocks the human's pairs only for his recorded duels and resolves his shots there from these reports (`docs/core.md`). No new action; the 2D engine emits it in `Sim2DHost.stepPawnWeapon` (the hitscan is unchanged), CS2 from the `OnTakeDamage` hook that blocks the pair (`docs/cs2-adapter.md`).
- **Takeover** (G4, `docs/core.md` "Takeover"). `observe.human` is no longer fixed for a run: null while the player watches (every pawn REPLAY, the no-human round), then the pawn he took control of from the tick it happened (the engine hands it over before that tick's observe). `take_control {id}` (`TakeControlControl`) is the app's request; it is engine and driver work like a checkpoint (2D: `Session.takeControl` → `Sim2DHost.setHuman`; CS2: the player's E press on the spectated bot, which the plugin allows for cart only), so it is not sent to the Core and not logged: the log shows the takeover as `human` changing in that line's observe, and a Core reacts to that alone (in CS2 nothing sends a control). On that tick the Core's Act has `unblock_damage` for the pawn's self pair and both directions with every enemy whose blocks aren't lifted (11 for cart), a `log` (`takeover: cart is the human from tick <k> (<hp> hp, <weapon>); <n> damage blocks lifted`) and its encounters' logs; no Core input for him from then on. `human` goes back to null only through a rewind to a checkpoint before the takeover (the Core and the engine are restored or resynced; the resync prefix makes the pawn REPLAY and blocks its pairs), or, CS2, when the player leaves without a rewind: the Core then makes the pawn LIVE with trigger `released` (its blocks stay lifted). A takeover at tick 0 is a human from the start, byte for byte. No new action, event or Observe field.
- **Summary** (B5). `RoundSummary` (`summary.ts`): `headline`, `verdict` (`tape same_result changed unfinished`), `result` / `recorded` (`RoundResult {winner, reason, endTick, score}`), `bomb` / `recordedBomb` (`BombSummary {plantTick, defuseTick, explodeTick, marginSec}`), `kills` (`KillRow {status: same|shifted|missed|pending|new, killer (null = the world), victim, weapon, headshot, recordedTick, runTick, deltaTicks, postRound, text}`), `switches` (`SwitchRow {id, tick, trigger, reason}`), `player` (`PlayerStats {id, human, kills, damageDealt, damageTaken, aliveSec, deathTick, rmsFromRecording, rmsTicks}`), `ticks`, `lastTick`. No optional fields.
- **PawnObs.onGround** (G3). `onGround: boolean`: the pawn stands on ground (Source's ground entity is set). `vel` was already a `Vec3`: with 3D movement `vel.z` is the vertical speed (up positive; 0 on the ground) and `pos.z` the feet height on the CS2 mesh (no longer the nav floor). A Core may use both; the puppets use `onGround` to time a jump. CS2: `m_hGroundEntity` valid, `m_vecAbsVelocity.z`.
- **JUMP** (G3). The `JUMP` button (already in `BUTTONS`) now jumps: a fresh press on the ground (the engine tracks whether it was held, Source's `m_nOldButtons & IN_JUMP`); held, it jumps once. The human presses it with Space; a puppet for a take-off sample (`docs/core.md` "Jumps"). CS2: `IN_JUMP` in the bot's `CUserCmd` on that tick.
- **TrackSample.jump** (G3). Optional `jump: 'plain' | 'crouch'` on a track sample (validated by `parseRound`): a jump takes off at this sample toward the next one (a crouch jump holds DUCK in the air). Set only by `applyTracks3d` from the snapped tracks' `jumps`; `round.json` has none, and the current snapped tracks have none either (`docs/geometry.md` "Track snapping").
- **Tracks3d** (G3, `src/contracts/tracks3d.ts`). `data/geometry/mirage-tracks.json` (`format` `cs2-round-replay/tracks3d@1`): `tracks[id]` one `[x, y, z]` per sample of `round.tracks[id].samples`, `jumps` `{id, t, tick, crouch}`, plus `note`, `sources` (SHA-256 of the inputs), `params`, `stats`. `parseTracks3d` validates it; `applyTracks3d(round, tracks3d)` returns the round with every sample moved there (and the jump flags set, and a player's `start.pos` moved with his first sample when they were equal). The app and the harness apply it before the Core sees the round; the recorded round stays available (`SessionData.recorded`).
- **WalkQuery in 3D** (G3).
